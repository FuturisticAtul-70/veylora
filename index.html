ABOUT VEYLORA

Where heritage meets the modern woman.

Veylora began with a simple belief: a woman should never have to choose between the traditions she loves and the life she lives. Our Indo-Western designs bring together hand-finished embroidery, flowing silhouettes and soft, breathable fabrics.

Every piece is made to move from a family celebration to an evening out, and to be worn, remembered and passed on. Crafted with care, priced with honesty, and backed by easy returns.
